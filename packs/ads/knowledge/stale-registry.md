# Stale registry: what changed

A platform mechanic that is gone, described as if it were live, is the most common way an ads agent is wrong. Each row here names what a mechanic was and what is current. Read it before stating any setup step. Mark every mechanic you name as current or historical.

| mechanic | what it was | what is current | source | verified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta eight-event priority flow | configure up to eight conversion events per domain, rank them 1 to 8, verify the domain, select a conversion domain per campaign, wait a processing period | removed. No events to prioritise, no aggregated-measurement tab in Events Manager, no domain verification step, no conversion-domain selection. Web events process automatically. Current mechanics: modeled conversions, aggregated measurement, SKAdNetwork's 0 to 63 conversion value, 1-day and 7-day click | Meta Business Help, "About Aggregated Event Measurement" | 2026-06-13, verify |
| Meta funnel-segmented account builds | separate campaigns by funnel stage (top, middle, bottom), each with its own audience | historical as a default. Meta's own cost guidance is to combine similar campaigns and ad sets so the delivery system has more signal; the common current shape is prospecting, retargeting and creative testing, with the creative doing the targeting. Treat an inherited funnel build as a migration target | Meta Business Help, "Best practices to potentially reduce cost per result for Meta ads" (facebook.com/business/help/321695409726523) | 2026-10-15 |
| Meta lookalikes as the structure | build the account around lookalike audiences from seed lists | fading. With Advantage+ audience, custom and lookalike audiences are suggestions the system may go beyond, not the structure; add one only as a suggestion and only when broad delivery misses | Meta for Business, "Meta Advantage+ audience" (facebook.com/business/ads/meta-advantage-plus/audience) | 2026-10-15 |
| Google Ads "Power Pair" | the paired automated campaign types | renamed "Power Pack": Performance Max, Demand Gen and AI Max; naming churns | Google Ads announcements, 2026 | 2026-02-27, verify |
| Google dynamic search ads | a standalone campaign type | being migrated into AI Max for Search; new dynamic campaigns already unavailable | Google Ads Help, "About AI Max for Search campaigns"; Google Ads announcements 2026 | 2026-05, verify |
| Google Ads recommended target as the default | accept the suggested target CPA or ROAS at launch | Google's own evaluation guidance is to measure the last 30 days with at least 30 conversions; set the target near your own stable recent results, or stay manual on a new account; the suggestion often sits above what the account has shown | Google Ads Help, "About Target CPA bidding" (support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6268632) | 2026-10-15 |
| X links in replies | links in the post body cut reach, so put them in the first reply | X's head of product said in 2025-10 that the drop came from the in-app browser covering reactions and that an iOS fix was live on 2025-10-25; on 2026-07-28 he said links need not go in replies; on 2026-07-29 X's owner said there had been no link penalty for over a year. These are staff posts on X, not a measured zero effect. Write the post to stand alone and test placement | posts on X by X's head of product (2025-10-25, 2026-07-28) and X's owner (2026-07-29) | 2026-07-29, verify |
| TikTok organic comment-to-message | parity with Instagram's comment-to-message lane | not at parity in the US, UK and EU as of mid-2026; a Business Messaging API exists, gated by region and partner | TikTok for Business, "Business Messaging" | 2026-06, verify |

## How to add a row

Add a row the moment a platform removes, renames or reverses a mechanic the pack names. Keep the old description so an agent that meets it in an old guide recognises it. Date the current state. Name the page or the dated post.
