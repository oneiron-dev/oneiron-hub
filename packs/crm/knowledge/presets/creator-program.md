# Preset: creator program

A stage ladder for recruiting, trialling and paying real creators. Creators are people with a contract, a trial and a payout ladder, so this is pack data for crm. The craft of hooks and formats lives in the ads pack. The economics (a CAC ceiling from price and lifetime gross profit) live in the campaign brief.

## The gate

A creator program is budget-heavy and time-heavy. It works best once demand and price are settled and one person owns running it. It wants to be the channel, not a side bet next to another paid channel.

## The ladder

| stage | evidence |
|---|---|
| `sourced` | a membership claim with the source and a dedupe check against prior outreach |
| `replied` | a coded positive reply |
| `onboarded` | the onboarding call held, the contract and tax forms signed |
| `trial` | the first post live inside the trial window |
| `graduated` | the trial bar cleared, or `cut` if not |
| `pay_per_post` | eligible posts paid on a rolling cycle |
| `retainer` | proven output moved to a fixed cycle |
| `exited` | a reliability or performance cut, done fast and never cruelly |

## Comp

- Derive pay from the CAC ceiling, never from someone else's dollar figures.
- A retainer is a views bet. Proven creators go on retainer; unproven ones stay per post.
- Payout eligibility goes in the contract before recruiting. A view floor per video, on one platform. A posts-per-week minimum. Bonuses that replace the base for that video. An eligibility window.
- Milestone bounties for breakout hits. Consistency bounties in the first months. A physical prize can beat cash.
- Count the all-in program cost: runner, editors, scripters, outreach help, payouts.

## Recruiting

- Recruit wide behind the trial. A creator who never lands costs almost nothing.
- The trial: a fixed window, one bar on one post, a flat reward, graduate or cut.
- Recruit on trial output, never on a casting type. Screening on protected traits is a legal risk.
- The runner sells the vision: growth roles, ready scripts, bounties, real examples.
- Sourcing menu: personal network, cold outreach on the platforms, creator communities, campus clubs and boards, referrals from current creators. Log every contact so nobody is approached twice.
- Unpaid for-credit interns pass only the primary-beneficiary test. See `platform-facts.md`. Credit alone is no safe harbour.

## Onboarding

- A short call: what the program is, how it works, what you need from them.
- The comms channel, then the contract and tax forms. Automate the signing.
- Creators keep their accounts. The brand gets content rights for paid use. Spell out the trial, comp, eligibility, liability, termination and payment timing.

## Comms and coaching

- Run comms where attention already is. A daily direction text with a read check.
- One warning for a missed minimum, then act.
- A posting window. A fixed coaching rhythm: a group call and a short one-to-one each week.
- Build once and reuse: a creator manual, a sounds library, product demo clips, a short content course.

## Content

- Post every video on every short-video platform.
- Research the niche before scripting. Each niche has its own speech patterns.
- Categories: entertainment builds reach and rarely converts; how-to-earn converts and gets restricted; a reaction plus a demo works without a presenter.
- Restricted income claims: fix the claim, not just the keyword.
- Account warm-up and shadow-ban signs are perishable folklore. Verify current behaviour; do not build process on them.

## Support roles

Editors for good creators only. Scripters. Coaches. Comment-to-DM through the platform's approved API. Affiliate codes read as a floor, never the total. Post tracking for fraud: spikes that do not match reach, low engagement against views.

## The disclosure floor

A paid or affiliate connection is disclosed clearly and conspicuously, in the video itself and above the fold. The brand instructs and monitors. Build it into the contract, the manual and the review list. The compliance rule rows will carry the jurisdiction detail once a creator-disclosure row exists; until then read each market's regulator page. No fabricated testimonials, no staged comments, no claims the product cannot deliver, no shame hooks.

## Verdicts

Judge each creator separately from the program. Cut or keep per creator, fast. Kill, fix or scale the program on retention-adjusted CAC and all-in cost, against gates written before launch.
