# Review and renewal

Templates for the recurring account rhythm. Fill them from the account's claims. Never a number you cannot point to. Call lengths and the renewal offsets are the pack's practice defaults in `defaults.md`; the brief overrides them.

## The quarterly review agenda, 30 minutes

| min | part |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | "When you started, the aim was {{goal}}. Still the main thing?" |
| 3 to 12 | before and after, in their numbers: {{metric}} from {{then}} to {{now}}; what drove it |
| 12 to 18 | issues, yours first; what slipped and why; what changes |
| 18 to 25 | (healthy accounts only) one observed gap and a specific idea; or the next quarter's plan |
| 25 to 30 | the renewal date, said out loud; the next checkpoint booked |

If the account is unhealthy, parts one to three are the meeting. Skip the growth ask.

## The health sheet

One row per account. The vault assembles it from claims.

| account | last contact | sentiment | key work done? | deliverables used? | sponsor active? | current scope | target scope | flags |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Two or more flags: reach out today.

## The at-risk reach-out (paying client gone quiet)

```
Subject: quick check-in on {{project}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I haven't wanted to crowd you, but I'd rather hear it straight: is {{project}} still delivering what you needed, or has something shifted on your side?

If it helps, I have {{one concrete next step}} ready when you are. If priorities changed, tell me and we'll work out the right move together.

{{sender_first_name}}
```

Escalates toward the truth. Warm, not needy. One concrete next step. Respects an honest exit.

## The renewal schedule

| when | what |
|---|---|
| 90 days out | the final review; surface blockers; make value visible |
| 60 days out | fix anything the review surfaced |
| 42 days out | the renewal conversation; terms; the expansion idea if healthy |
| 30 days out | paperwork in motion; a mutual plan for a bigger renewal |
| 14 days out | signature or a clear reason why not |
| the last weeks | momentum: a visible win, a shipped fix, a prompt reply to everything |

## The expansion line

```
While we were fixing {{the delivered thing}}, I noticed {{the observed gap}}. It's costing you about {{their figure, if measured; otherwise "something we could measure in a week"}}. I have an idea to {{the specific improvement}}. Want to hear it on Thursday?
```

One path, tied to evidence from the engagement. Never a menu.

## The referral ask

At the moment the numbers are on screen:

```
Can I ask one thing? Who are one or two people you know dealing with {{the specific problem}}? If a name comes to mind, here is a paragraph you can forward as is:

"{{sender_first_name}} helped us {{outcome in our units}} in {{time}}. If {{problem}} is on your plate, it's worth a short call: {{contact}}."
```

Frame it as a gift they give the other person.

## The testimonial questions (after delivery)

1. What nearly stopped you from going ahead?
2. What did you find once you did?
3. What was most useful?
4. What else changed?
5. Would you recommend it, and why?
6. Anything to add?

Open the written testimonial on the doubt from question one.
