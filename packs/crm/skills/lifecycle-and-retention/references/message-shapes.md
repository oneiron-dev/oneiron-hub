# Message shapes

Every SEND block: short, plain, respectful, one action, no guilt, no fake urgency, no invented numbers. The reasoning stays in PREP. Industries vary on purpose.

## Consumer win-back (the busy or disappointed segment), at expiry

```
Subject: the thing you asked for is ready

Hi {{first_name}},

A while back you wanted {{the specific thing they asked for}}. It works now, and what you said is part of why.

Everything you had is still there, where you left it. If now is a good time, picking up is one tap: {{one_link}}

If now is not the time, that is fine. Nothing to do.

{{sender_first_name}}
```

Why it passes: anchored on their own ask, one action, opting out is explicitly fine, a real person signs it, no deadline that is not real.

## A nudge aimed at the user's own progress

```
{{first_name}}, you have {{n}} days of {{their thing}} logged this month. One more today fills in {{month}}'s picture.

[Log it, one tap]
```

The loss named is their real pattern, not an app-centric threat. Suppress it the moment they have logged today. Back off after the taper window.

Banned form: "You'll lose your streak!" / "Your progress will be deleted in 24 hours."

## "What we shipped" (active subscribers)

```
Subject: three things that changed in {{product}} this month

Hi {{first_name}},

1. {{change, one line, what it lets you do}}
2. {{change}}
3. {{change}}

Next up: {{one thing}}. If you want it sooner, reply and tell me why.

{{sender_first_name}}
```

## The cancel flow (screens, not mail)

1. **One click to the cancel screen** from account settings. The label says "Cancel subscription."
2. **One question.** "What made you cancel?" Options: too expensive; not using it right now; couldn't get it working; missing something; bought by mistake; other.
3. **One matched response, or none.**
   - too expensive: the cheaper tier, or the annual reframe; one line; the cancel button stays
   - not using it right now: "Pause for {{pause_length}}?" and the cancel button
   - couldn't get it working: "Want a ten-minute setup call?" and the cancel button
   - missing something: "Tell me what" and the cancel button
   - bought by mistake: no offer; confirm the cancel and the refund route
4. **Confirm.** "Cancelled. You keep access until {{date}}. Your data stays for {{period}} if you come back."

No second confirmation. No countdown. No "are you sure you want to lose everything."

## The founder's cancel note (within minutes of a cancel)

```
Subject: what made you cancel?

Hi {{first_name}},

Even four words helps. What made you cancel {{product}}? I read every one of these myself.

{{sender_first_name}}
```

## Angry-user recovery

```
Hi {{first_name}},

You're right, and I'm sorry. {{What went wrong, in one plain sentence, no excuse.}} Here is what I've done: {{the fix}}. Here is what I'm adding on top: {{the repair beyond the refund}}.

If it happens again, reply to this and it comes straight to me.

{{sender_first_name}}
```
