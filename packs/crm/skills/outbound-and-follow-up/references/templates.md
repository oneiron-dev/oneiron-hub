# Templates

Shapes, not scripts. Keep what each line does; write the words for this person. Every SEND block below is under its cap and carries only `{{facts}}` the owner supplies. Industries in the examples are varied on purpose.

## Touch one: trigger, trouble, proof, tiny ask

```
Subject: {{first_name}}, the {{trigger_noun}}

{{first_name}}, saw {{the specific trigger: a post, a hire, a launch, a filing}}.

How are you handling {{the failure mode that trigger usually brings}} right now?

A {{similar customer by role or size}} had the same thing and {{outcome in their units}}.

Worth a look?

{{sender_first_name}}
```

Example, a veterinary group hiring its first overnight vet:

```
Subject: Dana, the night-shift posting

Dana, saw you're hiring a first overnight vet. Big step.

How are cases handed over between the day team and the night shift right now? One clinic runs on a whiteboard; a round-the-clock rota usually doesn't.

A three-site vet group we work with stopped losing overnight cases at the handover once the shift notes left the whiteboard.

Worth a look?

Priya
```

## Middle-line patterns

Pick one for the middle of a touch-one mail.

- **The deeper problem.** "The hard part isn't {{surface task}}. It's {{the deeper constraint}}."
- **Want but blocked.** "Most {{role}} want {{aspiration}} but {{blocker}}."
- **Before and after.** "A {{similar company}} had {{problem}}. Now they {{outcome}}."

## Three-block mail

Block one: a strong reason you are writing plus one line of familiarity. Block two: one sentence of authority, quantified and true. Block three: the ask, with a concrete availability window.

```
{{first_name}}, I'm writing because {{the thing of theirs you read or saw}} and I liked {{the specific point}}.

I run {{what you do, one clause}}; a {{similar customer}} {{true result}} last {{season}}.

Fancy a quick call this week? I'm usually free after 2pm your time.

{{sender_first_name}}
```

## The short recording

For a prospect with a public asset, record a short walkthrough of their own thing. Show, don't claim. The ask is their reaction, never a booking link.

```
{{first_name}}, I recorded a short walkthrough of {{their asset}} and what I'd change first. No pitch in it.

{{link only after they reply; in touch one say: "want me to send it?"}}

{{sender_first_name}}
```

## Marketplace proposal (under the cap in `voice-floor.md`)

Answer their post in their words. A micro-diagnosis. A small paid first step.

```
You wrote that {{their words}}. From your {{file, site, listing}} the first thing I'd check is {{specific observation}}, because {{one-line reason}}.

If that's right, a {{small first step}} would show it in {{time}}. {{price}}, and you'll know quickly whether the bigger job is worth doing.

{{sender_first_name}}
```

## Bumps (under the bump cap)

Same thread. One new sentence of value.

- "{{first_name}}, one more thing: {{a resource, a number, a question}}. Still worth a look?"
- "Any thoughts?" (late in the sequence only)

## Re-engagement (under the re-engagement cap)

- The honest close: "If this isn't useful I'd rather stop writing. A one-word reply either way is plenty."
- The status check after silence: "Is {{the project}} still moving on your side, or has it dropped off the list?"
- After slipped commitments: "{{Y}} was next after we agreed {{X}} mattered. It has slipped twice now. What changed on your side?"
- The re-open on a new trigger: "We spoke in {{month}}; the timing was off. I see you're still {{the signal}}, and we've since {{what changed}}. Worth a fresh look?"
- The warm-list wake-up: "Is {{the goal they named}} still on the list this quarter?"

## Champion who moved (under the cold-touch cap)

```
{{first_name}}, congratulations on {{new company}}. You know what {{the work}} did at {{old company}}; if your first months there have a version of that problem, I'd be glad to help you land a quick win. Want a short call once you've found the coffee machine?

{{sender_first_name}}
```

## Reconnect arm (warm lane, prior thread exists)

Name the real prior thread. Never pretend you are strangers.

```
{{first_name}}, we last spoke in {{month}} about {{the thread}}. {{What changed on your side or theirs}}. If it's live again, I'd pick it up where we left it.

{{sender_first_name}}
```

## Post-meeting note

Three takeaways in their words. Three next steps with an owner on each side and a date.

```
Thanks for today. What I heard:
1. {{takeaway}}
2. {{takeaway}}
3. {{takeaway}}

Next:
- {{you}}: {{step}} by {{date}}
- {{them}}: {{step}} by {{date}}
- both: {{step}} on {{date}}

Shout if I've got any of it wrong.
```

## The funnel's email spine

A funnel is unfinished without it. Defaults in `defaults.md`: about seven mails after an opt-in, four before a booked call, five after. Each carries one idea and one ask. The sequence is the program's steps; the brief holds the copy.

## Concrete times, never "when works for you?"

Every ask that leads to a meeting names a window or two slots. Each extra exchange leaks responders.
