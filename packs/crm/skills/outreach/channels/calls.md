# Calls: live, recorded and AI-voice

Checked 2026-10-21 for practice; the law per country was checked 2026-10-22. Every default here is one the owner can override; the override line records who and why.

## Loop default

- Manual live calls to researched business contacts.
- Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 in the called party's local time. This is a business-window choice, not a legal window.
- Accurate caller identity. No rotated or hidden caller id.
- Before dialling: the national do-not-call register where one exists, and the vault's own suppression claims.
- Log every outcome. Honour a requested callback.
- An AI agent that dials and speaks alone is an automated call everywhere. Consent is the default.

## Why phone at all

Phone runs beside email and LinkedIn for high-fit accounts and converts an interested reply into a conversation. Gong's 2024 recap associates calling with higher email reply rates (3.44% against 1.81%) and voicemails with higher rates still (5.87% against 2.73%). The dataset is 300 million calls, observed, not assigned at random. It supports testing phone beside email. It does not prove phone caused the lift. An opener that "works 80% of the time" in a sales forum is an anecdote, not a forecast.

## What changes the exposure

Five things are separate legal triggers in at least one country. The number type: mobile, residential or business line. Live voice against recorded or artificial voice. The dialling technology. Recording. The register state. The law file's two call sections are the record.

| recipient in | live calls | automated or AI voice |
|---|---|---|
| UK | screen against TPS and CTPS with a list no older than 28 days; honour objections; reg 24 identity and address on request | specific consent; reg 19(4) defines automated by "sounds which are not live speech" |
| EU | the member state chooses opt-in or opt-out | consent for natural persons; corporate scope is national |
| Germany | B2B: a narrow presumed-consent test (concrete facts suggesting the business wants the call); B2C: express consent, documented and kept five years | consent |
| US | live B2B calls to business lines are the open route; mobiles, the National DNC list and state laws apply; Oklahoma and Maryland cap calls at three per 24 hours, 8am to 8pm, with written consent for automated selection or dialling | prior express written consent; the FCC calls AI voices "artificial" |
| Japan | identify, state the purpose, never re-solicit after a no; B2B calls sit outside the consumer rules | consent for automated consumer calls; no statute read addresses AI voices (JP-17 open) |
| Australia | the Do Not Call Register is a separate regime (draft) | consent (draft) |
| draft countries | the country file's call sections | consent where the file is unsure |

## If you override

Off-hour calls and high dial volume reach more people and draw more complaints, unanswered calls and number-reputation problems. Law and carrier or dialler terms add their own consequences; the country file sizes them. Rotating caller ids to hide identity is a separate breach in several countries. An email address is not permission to call or text.
