# LinkedIn and other private social DMs

Checked 2026-10-21 for LinkedIn's own rules; the law per country was checked 2026-10-22. Every number here is a default the owner can override; the override line records who and why.

## How this pack sends on LinkedIn

The agent drafts. A human clicks each send in their own session (ARCH-0059 §9). Nothing connects to the person's account. The numbers below pace that human. An owner who runs an automation tool on their own account records that as an override; `../platforms/linkedin-tools.md` sizes the exposure.

## Three message buckets

| action | what LinkedIn publishes | loop default |
|---|---|---|
| connection invitations | limits exist for every member; no universal weekly number is published. Bursts, pending or ignored invitations, spam reports and suspected automation trigger restrictions. Most invitation restrictions clear in about a week; a backlog of pending invitations can take up to a month. Withdrawing an invitation blocks a new one to the same person for up to three weeks. | 5 researched invitations a day, 25 a week |
| ordinary messages | free to first-degree connections; some non-connections through Open Profile, coworker or group requests. LinkedIn limits messages in a period and publishes no number. | 10 new conversations a day; replies are not counted |
| InMail and Sales Navigator | 50 credits a month on Core, Advanced and Advanced Plus; at most 150 banked; a credit expires after 90 days. A reply within 90 days returns the credit, "not interested" included. A second InMail before a reply costs another credit. Extra Sales Navigator credits cannot be bought. | use the real credit balance and fit; no invented daily cap |

The "100 a week" figure is a vendor rule of thumb, not LinkedIn's number. Vendor products disagree with each other: one caps at 200 a week, another sells 300 or 800 a month.

## What draws a restriction

LinkedIn's help names more than automation: many invitations in a short period, invitations left pending or marked spam, and suspected automation. A person typing by hand can still be restricted. The user agreement (§8.2) bans bots and other unauthorised automated methods to access the service, add or download contacts, or send or redirect messages, and bans scraping. The help says members using prohibited tools risk being restricted or shut down. The sources give no restriction probability.

## Voice notes and SSI

Voice notes run up to one minute, in the mobile app only, to first-degree connections or group chats. A short personal note is a format choice when the conversation suits it. The Social Selling Index runs 0 to 100. No primary source says a score unlocks more invitations. LinkedIn's own page says a high score "doesn't always represent the efficacy of a sales person or correlate with measurable sales outcomes".

## The law on a DM

A marketing DM is electronic mail in the UK and the EU. The law file's section "LinkedIn and other private social DMs" is the record. In one line each:

| recipient in | the DM default |
|---|---|
| UK | consent for a member account on the strict reading; realistic exposure is low, with no ICO case found; many UK B2B senders DM on an opt-out basis, and that is the owner's call |
| EU | treated as electronic mail (Art 2(h); CJEU StWL); the member state sets the B2B rule |
| Germany | consent; an appellate court treats LinkedIn and Xing messages as electronic mail (OLG Hamm 2023) |
| US | add CAN-SPAM content: identification, an opt-out and a postal address; district-court authority only |
| Japan | outside the email statute on its face; the loop still identifies the sender and honours a stop |
| draft countries | the country file's DM section; consent where the file is unsure |

Add the UK and EU first-message line: they can object to direct marketing, with a privacy-notice link.

## If you override

More invitations or DMs can reach an account-specific limit and draw spam reports. A tool on the account is an account-restriction exposure, with no number in the sources. Sales Navigator, a high SSI and a country-matched IP change nothing. Buying an aged account breaks the one-real-name rule. The legal exposure follows the country file.
