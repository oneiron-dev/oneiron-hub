# Canada: outreach law
Researched 2026-10-22 (JST; 2026-10-21 UTC) by Sol from primary sources where they were reachable. Status: **draft, not yet confirmed by a second reader against the primary texts**. Each rule below is a lead with its source, not a confirmed statement; `../keeping-current.md` says how to confirm one. Every default is a starting value. The owner, or his agent within its grant, can override it; the override line records who and why. An override changes the chosen action, not the law. Scope: ordinary commercial B2B and B2C outreach. Source and version gaps are marked in the text.

**Bottom line:** CASL is consent-based, with narrow published-business-address and time-limited relationship branches.

## Channel defaults, legal reasons and override exposure

### Email

- **B2B default:** Express consent or a proven CASL implied-consent/exemption branch; for business email, conspicuous publication must meet every statutory condition.
- **B2C default:** Express consent; documented existing-relationship implied consent only while valid.
- **Default channel basis:** CASL6/10 express or limited implied consent; exact regulatory exemption if applicable.
- **Data-processing basis:** PIPEDA/provincial privacy rules independently.
- **Legal rule / uncertainty:** CASL commercial electronic messages include electronic addresses such as email, telephone accounts and instant-messaging accounts; private social messages can qualify. Conspicuous publication requires recipient-published address, no no-solicitation statement and relevance to business/role/functions/duties. A list broker’s republication or “on LinkedIn” alone is not proof. EBR commonly2 years after transaction/contract end,6 months after inquiry; preserve dates. Organisation-to-organisation exemption requires an existing inter-organisational relationship and relevant activities, not merely both parties being businesses.
- **Required content (qualified by scope/verification):** Identify sender and any person on whose behalf sent. Valid mailing address plus telephone/email/web contact; details and unsubscribe method functional for at least 60 days. Clear no-cost readily performed unsubscribe; a conspicuous linked page can supply required details where impracticable to include in message, subject to regulations.
- **Opt-out legal timing:** Without delay, at most10 business days after indication sent, without further action by recipient; loop suppresses immediately.
- **Loop handling:** Suppress further marketing on receipt, across the requested scope; recommended same-day handling. Keep a minimal suppression record, not the full discarded lead profile.
- **If overridden:** CASL administrative maxima are CAD1m for an individual/CAD10m for others per violation, subject to statutory factors. Consent burden is on sender; narrow publication evidence matters.
- **Evidence:** [CA-casl], [CA-regs]. Status: `mixed`.
- **Note:** A request for express marketing consent can itself be a CEM; it is not a general cold-message workaround.
- **Note:** Different social features (public broadcast versus address-directed private message) need classification.

### Live human telephone calls

- **B2B default:** Real B2B calls after internal DNC checks and general telemarketing-rule compliance.
- **B2C default:** DNCL/internal list screening, permitted hours and required registration/subscription; consent where an exception or listed number requires it.
- **Default channel basis:** CRTC Unsolicited Telecommunications Rules; B2B exemption from National DNCL is limited.
- **Data-processing basis:** PIPEDA/provincial rules.
- **Legal rule / uncertainty:** CASL is not the live-phone regime. B2B calls are generally exempt from the National DNCL screening branch, not from all telemarketing rules, identification or internal DNC duties. Detailed current CRTC text was security-blocked.
- **Required content (qualified by scope/verification):** Identify caller and principal, provide callback/contact, lawful caller ID, applicable local-time limits.
- **Opt-out legal timing:** Suppress immediately by recommendation; reported internal-list14-day processing/3 years14 days retention and DNCL31-day list cycle need current primary confirmation.
- **Loop handling:** Suppress further marketing on receipt, across the requested scope; recommended same-day handling. Keep a minimal suppression record, not the full discarded lead profile.
- **If overridden:** A DNCL exemption does not excuse misleading calling, ignored internal objections or invalid privacy sourcing.
- **Evidence:** [CA-phone]. Status: `primary_quote_unverified`.

### Automated / recorded / artificial-voice telephone calls

- **B2B default:** Express consent for solicitation using an ADAD; verify actual CRTC definition and exceptions.
- **B2C default:** Same default.
- **Default channel basis:** CRTC ADAD solicitation consent rules, not CASL email publication exception.
- **Data-processing basis:** PIPEDA/provincial rules.
- **Legal rule / uncertainty:** Prior express consent is generally required for automatic dialling-announcing solicitation, including B2B. Primary CRTC current rule text was blocked; do not assume a published business number supplies consent.
- **Required content (qualified by scope/verification):** Required caller/principal identity, callback and ADAD beginning-of-call disclosures; exact text to verify.
- **Opt-out legal timing:** Cease on withdrawal; current internal-list deadlines need confirmation.
- **Loop handling:** Suppress further marketing on receipt, across the requested scope; recommended same-day handling. Keep a minimal suppression record, not the full discarded lead profile.
- **If overridden:** Phone-rule administrative penalties and investigations; CASL10m email ceiling is not automatically the telephone fine.
- **Evidence:** [CA-phone]. Status: `primary_quote_unverified`.

## Shared content recommendation — not universal law

Use a truthful sender and company identity. Give a working reply/opt-out route; include a privacy-notice link when personal data is used. For email use a real postal/company address as a loop recommendation even where the channel law does not mandate one. Do not transplant this into a universal SMS/DM address requirement. These are defaults where the local statute does not expressly require the item. A physical postal address is not a universal SMS/DM requirement.

## Lead lists: collection, scraping and enrichment

PIPEDA applies to covered commercial personal-data activities; business-contact information has a limited exclusion when used solely for communicating in relation to employment/business/profession, not a universal enrichment exemption. Quebec private-sector law/Law25, Alberta and BC privacy law can differ. Track recipient publication, original URL/date, purpose and no-solicitation text. Buying a list does not prove the sender’s CASL consent. Federal CASL also amended privacy rules against address harvesting; exact PIPEDA/provincial texts are PRIMARY-QUOTE-UNVERIFIED here. [CA-casl; CA-regs]

Sources: [CA-casl], [CA-regs], [CA-phone], [CA-compufinder].

## Penalties and actual enforcement record

CASL20(4): CAD1m individual/CAD10m other-person maximum per violation. Criminal and privacy routes may be separate. CASL private right of action was suspended before commencement; current status must be confirmed rather than advertised as a private damages entitlement.

- **CompuFinder, CRTC2015 notice/2017decision — CAD1.1m proposed in2015; reportedly reduced to CAD200,000 in2017.** PRIMARY-QUOTE-UNVERIFIED: CRTC2017-368 security challenge; verify exact outcome and judicial history. Do not present the original notice as a final collected1.1m penalty. Sources: [CA-compufinder].

Statutory ceilings, announced settlements, original administrative decisions and money actually collected are different. No probability or expected-loss estimate is asserted. A large campaign/enrichment decision is not a tariff for one ordinary relevant B2B message. No claim of “no enforcement” follows from a missing case.
