# Clay

Checked 2026-10-21. Clay mainly supplies list research and enrichment, plus a sequencer. It does not replace the connected mailbox's policies.

## What the docs say

- About 20 a day per inbox; 3 inboxes per domain, 5 at most; a 20-minute minimum gap by default; about 3 weeks of warm-up, kept on.
- Text is the default; open and click tracking are off.
- Getting started says 5% bounces or more is the point to stop and inspect.
- The product cap is 10 to 500 a day, configurable; 30 for Clay-bought accounts.

## Reading it

Keep the recommendation (20 a day) apart from the product cap (up to 500). Clay's 5% bounce line is looser than the loop's 2%.

## Loop default

The loop's 20 a day matches Clay's recommendation. The loop's gap is 10 minutes; Clay's default is 20. Pooled warm-up off; Clay keeps it on. Bounce pause at 2%, not 5%.
