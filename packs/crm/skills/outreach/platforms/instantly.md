# Instantly

Checked 2026-10-21. A vendor page says what the vendor recommends and configures. It does not say what a receiving provider permits, and it proves no causal effect on placement.

## What the docs say

- Campaign settings recommend 30 campaign emails per account per day; 20 for Airmail accounts.
- The campaign limit spans the selected accounts. It is not 30 new leads plus unlimited follow-ups per inbox. Match account-wide limits across overlapping campaigns.
- Warm-up defaults: +1 a day, a 10-a-day limit, 30% simulated replies.
- Features: stop on reply; text-only and first-email-text-only modes; tracking toggles; unsubscribe header insertion; catch-all verification sold separately, with risky addresses off by default.
- The 2026 benchmark report: 3.43% average replies per email sent; top quartile 5.5% and above; top decile 10.7% and above; 58% of replies on step one, 42% later.

## Reading it

Simulated warm-up replies are not prospect replies and never advance a ramp. The unsubscribe-header feature contradicts the claim that headers destroy cold email. The benchmark divides all replies by all sends, across thousands of workspaces, with no clean positive-reply or list-type split; `../practice/benchmarks.md` holds the limits.

## Loop default

The loop starts at 20 a day per mailbox, below the vendor's 30, with pooled warm-up off. An owner on Instantly who prefers the vendor's numbers records the override.
