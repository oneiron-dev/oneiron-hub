# lemlist and lemwarm

Checked 2026-10-21. A vendor page says what the vendor recommends. It does not say what a receiving provider permits.

## What the docs say

- A global daily send limit covers campaign messages and follow-ups. It excludes lemwarm from its counter. The vendor says the total should stay at or under 50 a day; the example is 30 campaign plus 10 warm-up.
- The new-inbox warm-up article: a 30-to-40-a-day target, +1 a day, about 40 days of ramp plus 14 of stabilisation, then 10 a day residual.
- The vendor says a warm-up score does not predict campaign inbox placement.

## Reading it

Forty days plus fourteen is much longer than the "two weeks" slogan. A counter exclusion does not exempt warm-up traffic from the real provider's quota. The vendor's own statement about its score is the point to keep: a pool score is not placement.

## Loop default

Pooled warm-up off; a real-traffic ramp; 20 a day at steady state. An owner on lemlist who enables lemwarm records the override with its cost and the comparison they will read.
