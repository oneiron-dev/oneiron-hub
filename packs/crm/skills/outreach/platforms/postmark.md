# Postmark

Checked 2026-10-21. Postmark answers the contract question only; law and placement are separate.

## The terms

"All email lists … must be permission-based subscriptions." Purchased or rented lists are prohibited. Unsolicited messages are grounds for suspension or termination. Postmark now offers broadcast message streams beside transactional delivery; a different stream does not change the permission rule.

Published quality limits: complaints under 0.1%, bounces under 10%, with pause and cancellation as remedies.

## Volume

Plan- and credit-based. No published daily cold ceiling exists; do not invent one.

## Loop default

Requested transactional and opted-in messages only. The loop's bounce line is 2%; the 10% contract line is not a target.

## If you override

A cold list on Postmark risks a pause or cancellation. Reputation suffers well before 10% bounces.
