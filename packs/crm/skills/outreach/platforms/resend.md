# Resend

Checked 2026-10-21. The acceptable use policy is dated 2026-10-13. Resend answers the contract question only; law and placement are separate.

## The terms

"You are prohibited from sending unsolicited messages of any kind, including cold outreach, purchased lists, or scraped contact data." All mail goes to explicitly opted-in recipients. The policy requires complaints under 0.08% and bounces under 4%, and says accounts may be shut down without warning above those rates. The opt-in rule applies even when both rates are zero. Refunds are not issued for violations.

## Quotas

| plan | limit |
|---|---|
| free, transactional | 100 a day on a UTC calendar day; 3,000 a month; inbound counts |
| paid, transactional | no daily quota; the monthly plan quota remains |
| API | 10 requests a second, not 10 emails a second; excess returns 429 |

Batch and API accounting differ; read the quota page for the plan in use.

## Loop default

Not a cold route. Resend carries transactional and opted-in mail.

## If you override

A cold campaign through Resend can end the account without warning. A quota is not permission.
