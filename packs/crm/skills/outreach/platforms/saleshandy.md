# Saleshandy and TrulyInbox

Checked 2026-10-21. A vendor page says what the vendor recommends.

## What the docs say

- The daily-limit guide starts at 10 to 15 a day, with about 50 a day as the recommended maximum.
- The deliverability guide recommends TrulyInbox warm-up, several domains, 3 to 5% replies, under 3% bounces, intervals of 90 to 180 seconds minimum and 120 to 290 seconds recommended.
- It promotes 40 to 70% open rates.

## Reading it

These are vendor targets, not benchmarks. An open target is weak evidence: Apple Mail Privacy Protection fetches images without a human read (`icloud.md`).

## Loop default

Slower pacing (10 minutes), pooled warm-up off, open tracking off, bounce review at 2%.
