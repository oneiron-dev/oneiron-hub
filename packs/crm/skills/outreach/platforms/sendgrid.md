# SendGrid

Checked 2026-10-21. SendGrid answers the contract question only; law and placement are separate.

## The terms

Twilio's email policy: except for transactional email, "you must obtain affirmative consent prior to sending any emails". It bans unsolicited bulk mail, sends to scraped or public addresses without consent, and purchased or rented lists. It names snowshoeing, spreading traffic across domains or IPs to evade reputation controls, as prohibited.

## Quotas

A trial allows 100 a day for 60 days, then a paid plan. Paid volume depends on the plan and the account.

## Loop default

Not a cold route. A legally permitted B2B message is not automatically allowed by the contract.

## If you override

Review, restriction or suspension. Passing a trial send or buying a plan does not change the opt-in terms.
