# Twilio SMS

Checked 2026-10-21. The one messaging provider read. Other business-messaging products have their own terms; read them before transferring this one. The channel view is in `../channels/sms.md`.

## The messaging policy

- All Messaging Services traffic is application-to-person, whoever pressed Send.
- Prior express consent for any message. Prior express written consent for promotional messages.
- Selling, renting or transferring consent is prohibited. A purchased phone list carries none.
- The first message identifies the sender and offers "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" or an equivalent keyword.
- A question the user sent allows a relevant reply, not an indefinite marketing sequence.
- Campaign registration and a registered number are not consent.

## Loop default

Cold-list SMS off. SMS when the recipient asked for it or agreed, with the proof on the member record.

## If you override

The loop records the owner's choice to text cold. The provider and the carriers still filter, reject and enforce on the account. The country's law file sizes the legal exposure separately.
