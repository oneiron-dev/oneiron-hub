# Woodpecker

Checked 2026-10-21. A vendor page says what the vendor recommends.

## What the docs say

- The provider-limit guide warns that high official quotas do not make sending more than 100 a day wise.
- The warm-up guide recommends manual real exchanges. New domains: 2 to 3 months, about 12 weeks. A new mailbox on an established domain: 3 to 4 weeks. It says 50 opening emails a day are enough and warns that follow-ups overlap with them.
- It suggests broad working-hour windows such as 08:00 to 18:00, and turning warm-up off before a large campaign to avoid combined-volume problems.

## Reading it

Do not convert "opening emails" into all-message capacity. Woodpecker's new-domain advice is far longer than other vendors', and its pool-off-at-launch advice conflicts with Clay's keep-it-on. The disagreement is not resolved by picking the loudest number; it is a reason to measure.

## Loop default

A real-traffic ramp with no mandatory domain age; pooled warm-up off; 20 a day at steady state. Send windows in `../practice/send-windows.md`.
